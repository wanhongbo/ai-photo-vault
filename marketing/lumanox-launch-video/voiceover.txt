LumaNox is a private photo and video vault for Android, built for moments when your camera roll gets sensitive.

Import selected media into an encrypted local vault, protected by a six digit PIN, biometric unlock, and automatic lock when the app leaves the foreground.

Then use local AI tools to review sensitive content: IDs, cards, QR codes, faces, chat screenshots, and text-heavy images, without turning your vault into a cloud workflow.

Before sharing, redact with mosaic, blur, black bars, white bars, oval blur, or manual regions.

LumaNox also helps classify media, spot blurry photos and near duplicates, and create encrypted backups you can restore with your PIN.

No ads. No vault media cloud upload. Just private media, under your control.

Try LumaNox on Google Play.
