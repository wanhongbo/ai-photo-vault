LumaNox is a private photo and video vault for people who want their sensitive media to stay local.

Open the vault, import only what needs protection, and keep it behind encrypted storage, PIN unlock, and biometrics.

The AI assistant runs on your device. It can help review sensitive items, find private details, classify media, and spot cleanup candidates.

The private camera saves straight into the vault, so new photos and videos do not have to pass through your normal camera roll.

Settings are built around control: subscription, security, backup, storage, language, and support are all easy to find.

No ads. No vault media cloud upload. Just your private media, under your control.
