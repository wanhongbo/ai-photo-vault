## Reddit Post
Title: I built an offline-first private photo vault with local AI redaction and sensitive media review

Body:
I have been building LumaNox, a private photo/video vault for Android focused on local encryption and practical privacy workflows.

The idea is not just "hide photos behind a lock". The app is built around the moments where a camera roll gets risky: importing sensitive media, finding IDs/cards/QR codes/chat screenshots/faces, redacting before sharing, cleaning blurry or duplicate media, and creating encrypted backups without handing the vault to a cloud sync service.

Current highlights:
- Encrypted local vault for photos and videos
- PIN + biometric unlock + automatic background lock
- Local AI sensitive review
- Mosaic/blur/black-bar/white-bar redaction before export
- Smart cleanup and classification
- Encrypted backup and restore
- No ads and no vault media cloud upload

I would love feedback from privacy-conscious Android users, especially around which workflows feel trustworthy or still need clearer UX.
